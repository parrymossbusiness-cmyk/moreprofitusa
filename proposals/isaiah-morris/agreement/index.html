03 What you receive

Five parts. One engine.

1

The Flagship Digital Hub

Custom built and deployed

We are engineering a dedicated, fast, mobile-first flagship site that acts as your central conversion asset, not a templated brokerage page.

  • Entity Architecture: A custom landing experience branded under your visual identity (Rich Blood / Isaiah Morris), designed to convert traffic from search engines and social media.
  • Conversion Paths: Direct click-to-call, calendar booking and structured pre-qualification forms, like "Are You Mortgage-Ready? Check Your Numbers."
  • Search and Generative Engine Optimization: Structured JSON-LD schema linking your business, local service areas (Solano County, Oakland, San Francisco, Sacramento) and your public profiles, so AI search engines recognize your expertise.
  • Interactive Guides and Content: Mobile-optimized guides to your key cities, products and services, including your New Construction Guide and your videos. It serves as a one-of-a-kind source for your brand that elevates how your target buyers see you, and helps search engines trust and recommend you.

Pre-qualification forms help buyers check their readiness. Final loan approval always comes from the buyer's lender.

2

Google Business Profile Optimization

Setting up and claiming your local presence to capture organic local searches in your primary territory. Add reviews, and this becomes one of the most powerful organic search signals you can have.

3

The 30-Second "Real Talk" Mini-Series

Content engine

Real estate decisions happen after buyers build trust. We take the guesswork out of your content by scripting a high-impact, short-form video series for Instagram Reels, Facebook, YouTube Shorts and LinkedIn.

  • Strategic Scriptwriting: Every script answers a direct customer question, pain point or financial hurdle in 30 to 45 seconds. Topics include:
    • Overtime pay calculations and union pre-approvals
    • Why verifiable tax returns matter before you look at properties
    • Down payment truths vs. real estate myths in Northern California
    • The path to your first home: meeting the agent halfway
  • Product Placement Integration: Natural framing within your recording spaces and lofts, with subtle Rich Blood apparel placement (hats, socks, tees) to reinforce your brand identity without distracting from the message.
  • Batch-Filming Blueprint: You spend 45 to 60 minutes every couple of weeks reading structured teleprompter scripts on your phone. We provide the hooks, bodies, calls to action and frameworks that lead to more viewer retention and shares, the metrics needed to reach more viewers.
4

Authority Carousels and Text Posts

  • Weekly educational graphics breaking down local market data, credit readiness and the math behind monthly mortgage payments.
  • Plain-talk breakdowns that establish you as the go-to financial educator for first-time and move-up buyers.
5

Lead Handoff and Conversion Workflow

  • DM keyword automation: Viewers comment or DM a keyword, like KEYED or NUMBERS, to instantly receive a homebuyer readiness guide or an intro link.
  • Elimination of tire-kickers: Leads reach out already educated on the need for verifiable income and serious intent.
