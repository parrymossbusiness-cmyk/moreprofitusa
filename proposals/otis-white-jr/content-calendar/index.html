04 Filming

Seven sessions, every Tuesday

You film 7 reels and one long-form video each Tuesday. Reels post starting that Thursday. The long-form posts the next Monday.

    Film the opening 3 times

    Each reel gets 3 takes of the first line. We test the extra takes to see which opening keeps the most people watching.

    Pick the right spot

    Home works for most scripts. When a script is about a place, film there if you can. In the car, stay parked with the engine off.

    Check every script

    Read each script before you film and change anything that doesn't fit your license or how you talk. You have the final say.
